A Chrome extension that turns news into poetry

What it does

Newspoem automatically transforms news articles into blackout poems. Open any supported news site and the extension redacts words in real time, leaving behind hidden poetry in the text.

Hover any blacked-out word to peek. Click "show all" to read the full article. Click the poem chip to reroll a new poem.

Features

Smart mode picks coherent, poetic phrases. Random mode is more chaotic. Adjust how many words survive with the slider. Every article, every visit — a new poem.

Works on 17 major news sites. No accounts, no servers, no data collection. Everything runs locally in your browser.
